About

I hold a PhD in Computer Engineering. Over my career I have worked on more than 100 software projects across many industries, in business analysis, testing, quality and project management roles.

For many years I have designed and delivered certification-focused training and mentoring programs. I have authored more than ten published books in Turkish on software, business analysis and agile practices.

My current focus is turning that experience into open, free resources: exam guides and practice exams for candidates preparing for international certifications, and concept books for readers who work with software but do not write code.
